# What to do in Zenith War (Wasteland King)

<!-- ai-translation-notice -->

> This page was translated with AI. The Russian version is the canonical text.

**Updated:** 2026-09-09

This guide is intended for young states. As a state develops and grows stronger, the way the event is organized may change.

The event takes place every other Saturday. It starts at 13:00 Moscow time (10:00 UTC).

## Earning points

There are three ways to earn points in this event:

1. Hold the Techno Center (TC) or turrets.

2. Kill enemy troops.

3. Lose your own troops.

You need 10,000 points to receive the maximum reward.

## Registration

Registration takes place during the week of Zenith War. The clan leader or the person responsible for the event sends out a link to a Google registration form.

Make sure you fill it in. Respect other people's work: registration saves the organizers a great deal of time.

The collected information will be used to draw up lists of people who will hold the Techno Center and turrets.

## Holding the Techno Center

Points for holding the TC are awarded for being in a turret or in the TC itself. You simply need to stay there.

The entire state is involved in holding the TC, but doing so from a single alliance is more convenient because it prevents the turrets from attacking the TC.

Therefore, on the day of the event, people planning to earn holding points join one state alliance and remain there for the duration of the event.

## Which turret to go to

Lists are published on the day of the event. They specify who goes to which turret. Send only the troop type stated in the list. Even if you cannot fill your entire march with the required troop type, simply send as many as you can.

Once you have enough points, do not immediately leave the turret. There is usually a schedule you need to follow.

Our shared task is to hold the TC and turrets. If everyone leaves as soon as they have secured their personal benefit, the state will struggle to hold the objectives.

If you are unsure whether you can leave yet, ask in chat.

## What to do if you are the captain

Decide which turret you will go to. How to do this is described above.

Your task is to activate temporary boosts and reach the turret as quickly as possible. After that, activate the Superboost.

Temporary boosts:

- attack and defense buffs;
- the Commander Talent Mega March;
- the Commander Talent First Aid;
- War Frenzy — you can activate it by scouting someone's HQ.

## How to earn kill points

When you are in a turret or the TC, people attack you and you kill the attackers. This earns points. Most of the time, simply staying in a turret lets you earn all the kill points you need.

If you could not earn enough points, you can kill players from other states. However, you must respect the NAP.

Usually, attacking players from other states and attacking their resource tiles is allowed only in the black soil. However, the rules may differ because agreements vary. On the day of Zenith War, make sure you find out whom you may and may not attack.

Once agreements are reached, the organizers send an alliance mail and post the terms in the bulletin. Look there before asking in chat.

## How to earn loss points

You earn loss points when your troops die or enter Deep Healing. Troops sent to the regular Infirmary do not earn points.

You therefore need to prepare in advance and fill the regular Infirmary with Taxis. If your Infirmary capacity is larger than the number of troops you can fill it with, it is better not to pursue loss points.

Fill the Infirmary exclusively with Taxis or other cheap troops. After the battles for the TC, you will need to heal your troops immediately, and healing Taxis is very cheap.

## How to fill the Infirmary

Usually, before Zenith War, someone sends their army to a resource tile so you can attack it with Taxis. This is how you kill your Taxis.

There are no deaths on resource tiles — only wounded troops. So however many Taxis you send, that many will go to the Infirmary.

It is important to attack a resource tile, not a camp or HQ. Only resource tiles have no deaths.

Fill the Infirmary while using the Balance talent tree, because this tree provides the largest squad size.

## How to kill your own troops and earn points

You usually need to attack other states unless instructed otherwise.

Most of the time, a NAP will be agreed upon. Its terms will be posted in the alliance bulletin and repeated in alliance mail.

Attack turrets and the TC in accordance with the terms of this NAP.

If the NAP says to remove your equipment, chips, and other boosts and attack with a squad of no more than 50,000 troops, do exactly that. Otherwise, you will put your state in a difficult position and cause many problems.

## What to do if you accidentally capture a turret or the TC

If you accidentally capture a turret or the TC while a NAP is in effect, immediately withdraw your troops and write in division chat that it was an accident.

## How many troops to kill and which ones

Kill troops of the highest tier available to you. Kill only as many as needed to earn the maximum number of points. There is no need to kill more.

## How to avoid losing your army

You can be attacked while in the black soil. If you remove your equipment, chips, and other boosts there, your army may be destroyed beyond recovery. Therefore, do not remain in the black soil while doing this.

Wounded soldiers first go to the Infirmary. Once it is full, they enter Deep Healing, and once that is full, they go to the Alliance Infirmary. If the Alliance Infirmary is full, wounded troops die permanently.

If you leave the alliance while your troops are in the Alliance Infirmary, they will die permanently.

## When to heal Taxis

Once you have earned enough loss points, it is best to immediately heal all the Taxis you used to fill the Infirmary.

There are two reasons for this:

1. To prevent newly wounded troops from entering Deep Healing. Troops there are harder to heal.

2. To make Nanomedicine recover faster.

## Capturing the TC and the end of Zenith War

Capturing the TC does not end the event: it lasts a total of 24 hours. You can be attacked in the black soil throughout that time.

Therefore, until Zenith War has fully ended, do not remain in the black soil without a shield or gather resources there, so that your army is not destroyed.

Destroying your army in the black soil earns points for the enemy, which can negatively affect your state's overall reward.
