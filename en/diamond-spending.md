# When and What to Spend Diamonds On

<!-- ai-translation-notice -->

> This page was translated with AI. The Russian version is the canonical text.

**Updated:** 2026-09-09

## The best time to spend diamonds

The best time to spend diamonds is during the Diamond Expert event. It takes
place every other week, on Saturday and Sunday. You can check the calendar to
see whether the event is happening this week.

During this event, you should raise your VIP level—up to VIP 13. There is no
need to invest more diamonds in VIP after that. In short, VIP 13 lets you fully
complete the shovel event without spending real money.

You can also buy AP—Action Point—containers during Diamond Expert. They may be
useful for farming books and participating in various events.

However, until you reach VIP 13, try not to spend diamonds unless necessary.

One of the following events may be running at the same time:

- the Diamond Shop, where you can find useful items;
- the Wasteland Mine, which can also be worthwhile to participate in.

Until you reach VIP 13, though, it is better to save your diamonds and not spend
them on these events.

## What else you may need diamonds for

Here is what I spend diamonds on:

- I buy Directed Relocates in the Mystery Shop. They are always useful, and you
  can buy them there at a discount.
- I increase seed rarity in the Greenhouse. Making every seed gold will be too
  expensive for many players, but you can use a few attempts.
- I claim additional resources at the Supply Depot, but only when there is a
  corresponding task in the Greenhouse.
- Once a day, I speed up the production of Nanoweapon Parts. It costs only 200
  diamonds per day and lets me produce weapons without interruption.
