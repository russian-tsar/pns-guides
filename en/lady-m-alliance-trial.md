# Why Lady M is useful in Alliance Trial

<!-- ai-translation-notice -->

> This page was translated with AI. The Russian version is the canonical text.

**Updated:** 2026-09-09

When the boss's paralysis wears off, Lady M can stop it from attacking.

First, use Mars's ability to reset the energy the boss has accumulated to zero. Then use Lady M's ability: for five rounds, the boss will be unable to recover energy, which means it will be unable to attack. You can keep dealing damage throughout that time.

If Lady M accumulates enough energy to use her ability again within those five rounds, you can extend the effect that prevents the boss from recovering energy. This gives you even more time to deal damage.
