## Why VIP 10 matters

The key benefit of VIP 10 is the ability to reset Commander Talent sets for
free. This feature can significantly strengthen your account in different
aspects of the game.

## How to use it

1. Open the Talents window by tapping the book icon in the lower-right corner.
   This is the window that contains Hunt Mastery, Mega March, and other
   talents.
2. Tap the eagle icon next to the **Use** button to open Talent Memory.
3. Tap the gear icon to configure a preset that you can quickly activate later.

## Which presets to configure

You will need three presets:

- one with the Economy talent tree fully upgraded;
- one with the Military talent tree fully upgraded;
- one with the Balance talent tree fully upgraded.

Switch between them depending on the situation.

## Which branch to use

### Economy

Use the Economy talent tree when starting construction or research, or when
sending marches to gather resources.

### Military

Use the Military talent tree when fighting—for example, during Valiant Conquest
or Zenith War—sending a squad to the Pit, participating in Apex Match, or
doing anything else that requires your army.

### Balance

Use the Balance talent tree at all other times. It speeds up healing and unit
training and increases stamina recovery speed.
