# Which heroes to take in your squad

<!-- ai-translation-notice -->

> This page was translated with AI. The Russian version is the canonical text.

**Updated:** 2026-09-09

I have noticed that many players do not choose the most effective heroes for their squads. I suspect this happens because it is not always obvious whom to include and whom to leave out.

I will try to explain the subject.

First, a caveat: this article is intended for the early stages of the game. You will acquire more heroes over time, but hopefully by then you will understand the basic principles of choosing them.

Above all, remember that you can reset heroes. It costs only 150 diamonds and returns all the serum and books you spent. If you upgraded an unnecessary hero by mistake, feel free to reset them.

You can test squad damage on Zombie Zero in the Hall of War.

## Basic rules for choosing heroes

Never bring economy heroes in a combat squad. They do not help in battle, because all their bonuses work passively and do not require the hero to be in the squad.

A hero's usefulness is determined by their squad skills. When choosing, consider the following:

- the more attack a hero provides, the better;
- the hero's skills should match your single-type squad or provide a bonus to the entire squad;
- among suitable heroes, choose the one with the larger bonus.

The hero's number of stars does not matter on its own.

For example:

- 2★ Maverick enhanced to +5 provides 10% Rider Attack;
- 5★ Requiem enhanced to +1 provides 9.7% Squad Attack;
- Maverick's bonus is higher, so in this case he will be more useful despite having fewer stars.

## How far to level up heroes

The level needed to unlock all squad skills depends on a hero's number of stars:

- a 2★ hero unlocks all squad skills at level 31;
- a 3★ hero at level 71;
- 4★ and 5★ heroes at level 131.

There is no point leveling heroes beyond these levels unless you use them in the Arena or plan to create nanoweapons for them.

Below, I will discuss specific heroes and give general recommendations. Keep in mind, however, that the best lineup depends on your heroes' enhancement levels: +1, +2, +3, and so on.

Over time, try to enhance every hero you use in your squad to at least +5.

## Heroes with squad-wide bonuses

Heroes with squad-wide bonuses suit any troop type because they provide bonuses to the entire squad. However, they need higher enhancement levels: squad-wide bonuses are usually lower than bonuses specialized for a particular troop type.

For the squad-wide hero slot, players usually choose a character whose troop type differs from their squad's main type. This is due to how strikes work in battles. For example, if you play Fighters, it is better to use a Shooter or Rider as your general-purpose hero.

A partial list of heroes with squad-wide bonuses: Nam Hyeol, Tomoyo, Requiem, Marlowe, Meyers.

There are heroes with squad-wide bonuses below 5★, but they are not worth considering.

What you should know about these heroes:

- at +4, they provide 25.6% attack and can replace 3★ heroes enhanced to +5;
- at +6 and promoted to 6★, they provide 51.2% attack and can replace 4★ heroes enhanced to +5.

## Heroes for Fighters

A starting squad without 5★ heroes: Noct, Sanguine, Seo Yeon, Barbie, Queenie.

Over time, you will acquire Ulrik and Zephyr. They can replace Queenie and Barbie.

You can replace Noct once you have a general-purpose hero enhanced to +4.

## Heroes for Riders

A starting squad without 5★ heroes: Maverick, Dr. Grey, Percival, Dom, Rain.

Over time, you will acquire Fox and Obsidian. They can replace Maverick and Dr. Grey.

You can replace Percival once you have a general-purpose hero enhanced to +4.

## Heroes for Shooters

A starting squad without 5★ heroes: Amber, Malcolm, Bard, Griffin, Hartwell.

Over time, you will acquire Ephraim and Phoenix. They can replace Amber and Bard.

You can replace Malcolm once you have a general-purpose hero enhanced to +4.
