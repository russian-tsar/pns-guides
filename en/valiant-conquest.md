# Valiant Conquest

<!-- ai-translation-notice -->

> This page was translated with AI. The Russian version is the canonical text.

**Updated:** 2026-09-09

Valiant Conquest takes place every other Saturday.

The event starts at 13:00 Moscow time (10:00 UTC) and lasts 24 hours.

## Preparation

During Valiant Conquest, you can be looted and your entire army can be destroyed. Dead units will be restored for free after the event ends, but you should still prepare for it.

1. Activate a shield before the event starts so that you cannot be attacked first. A short two-hour shield will do.

2. Once the event starts, activate a 24-hour shield to stay safe until it ends.

Remember: if you attack another player or a resource tile, your shield will disappear.

Also, the shield does not protect your squads on resource tiles. They can still be attacked and destroyed there.

## Participation

There are three ways to help your alliance and state:

1. Do nothing and stay under a shield. This prevents players from other states from scoring points off you.

2. Gather resources in other states.

3. Destroy the armies of players from other states.

The second and third methods earn points, increasing your personal rewards as well as your alliance's and state's rewards.

## Gathering resources

You need to gather resources in other states. You can travel there through the event window.

The maximum number of resource-gathering points counted toward alliance rewards is 7,500,000.

You can gather any resource type. Diamond mines are also available during this event.

Resource tiles in the black soil usually face heavy competition, so you do not have to gather there. You can settle a little farther away and occupy level-five or level-six mines.

## Destroying other players' armies

This is straightforward: destroy the army of a player from another state and earn points.

Important: only units belonging to players from other states count. Fighting players from your own state earns no points.

The maximum number of army-destruction points counted toward alliance rewards is 15,000,000.

However, it makes sense to earn more. This lets you rank higher in the personal standings and increase your state's reward.

Another benefit is the opportunity to take resources from other players. Your dead army will be restored for free after the event, while the captured resources will remain yours permanently.

## Safety

If you decide to destroy other armies, remember: your shield disappears after you attack, leaving your Headquarters vulnerable.

Stronger players can attack you and destroy your entire army.

To evade an attack, use a Directed Relocate. It lets you escape an attack that has already been launched at you. A Random Relocate will not help in this situation.

Prepare enough Directed Relocates or diamonds to buy them in advance.

## How to tell whether a player has an army

To determine whether a player has an army without scouting, look at their Might. If their current Might matches their Highest Might, they have no army.

You can view Highest Might by tapping the icon next to the player's current Might.

## When to destroy other players' armies

The best time to hunt other armies is right at the start of the event. Toward the end, many players have already lost their armies, making suitable targets harder to find.

The best order of action is to fight first, then switch to gathering resources.
